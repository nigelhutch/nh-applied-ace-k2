## Summary

This technical note documents the transition from the September 2026 governed-autonomy baseline to what is designated the **Second Major ACE Capability Era**.

The constitutional architecture remains the same: the Source Goal remains authoritative, model output remains a proposal rather than proof, qualified capabilities provide bounded effects, and ACE’s independent evaluator/controller mechanisms retain terminal authority over qualification and acceptance.

What changed substantially is the operating system around that architecture. Sustained work on real engineering projects exposed weaknesses in leases, maintenance, transport, recovery, reconciliation, worker/provider behaviour, stagnation handling and evidence continuity. Those weaknesses were increasingly converted into deterministic control mechanisms rather than additional instructions to the model.

A defining characteristic of the second era is the development path. Most capability growth emerged from governed autonomous operation on real projects, with ACE contributing to diagnosis, implementation, testing, evidence preservation and controlled promotion. The governing boundaries and authority model had already been established before September and remained unchanged; subsequent repairs and improvements did not require case-by-case human direction or approval.

The worker or Manager that proposes or implements a change does not gain authority to accept it. Qualification and terminal acceptance remain with ACE’s independent evaluator/controller mechanisms.

## Defining property

> ACE has increasingly become an instrument of its own engineering development: most capability improvements have emerged from governed autonomous operation, while authority to permit, qualify and accept those improvements remains with ACE’s independent evaluator/controller mechanisms rather than with the worker that proposed or implemented them. No case-by-case human approval is required.

## Scope

This document is a capability-era comparison against the September 2026 ACE K2 baseline. It does not replace the independently frozen September public claim set, and it is not a claim of unrestricted self-governance or unrestricted self-modification.
